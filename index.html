Questions

Do you keep or reuse my episode?

No. We use it only to make your folder, and delete the files after delivery unless you ask us to keep them for edits.

Is it AI or a person?

AI does the transcription, the cuts and the first drafts. Then every clip goes through a quality check (captions, framing, audio level, no cut-off words) before it's sent. Anything that fails gets redone.

What languages?

English for now.

What if I don't like the clips?

Tell us what's off and we redo them once, free. On the free episode there's nothing to lose.
